Delicious Peach Muffins

Back in 2019, one of my family's favorite restaurants, Folks' Southern Kitchen, closed down in Marrieta, GA. They had some of the best fried chicken but alos had the best Peach lemonade and Peach muffins that I have ever tasted! Every since they closed, I have been trying to figureout how to recreate those same delicious muffins. So here is my copycat recipe so far as I plan to continue refining it in the future.

Ingredients

Prep Time - 15 minutes

Cook Time - 30 minutes

These ingredients will make 12 regular muffins

  • 2 cups - Flour
  • 1 cup - Sugar
  • 1/2 tsp - Salt
  • 1 tsp - Baking Powder
  • 1/2 tsp - Baking Soda
  • 1/2 cup - Melted Butter
  • 2 - Eggs
  • 1/2 cup - Sour Cream
  • 1 cup - Chopped Peaches
  • 1/2 cup - Peach Juice

Instructions

  1. Go ahead and preheat your oven to 400°F and prep you muffin tin.
  2. Mix together eggs, butter, sugar, sourcream, and peach juice. Set to the side.
  3. Combine the dry ingredients in a separate bowl.
  4. Sift your dry ingredients into the wet ingredients as you mix
  5. Slowly add your chopped peaches to the batter and fold together until well combined.
  6. Spoon muffin batter into greased or paper-lined muffin cups, filling each cup about 2/3 full.
  7. Bake in the preheated oven for 25-30 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center of a muffin comes out clean.
  8. After taking them out of the oven, sprinkle them with sugar.
  9. Allow the muffins to cool in the pan for a few minutes before transferring them to a wire rack to cool completely.
  10. Enjoy your delicious peach muffins! They taste great warm or cold and make a great snack or breakfast item.
